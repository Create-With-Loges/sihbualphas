In Simple Words

What does SkillPulse AI actually do?

Behind the advanced machine learning and complex architecture, our platform solves real-world problems for four key groups of people.

👨‍🎓

For Students & Job Seekers

Tired of studying outdated topics? We analyze thousands of live job postings to tell you exactly what skills companies are hiring for right now. We predict your chances of getting hired and recommend short bridge-courses to make you the perfect candidate.

🎓

For Teachers & Institutes

We take the guesswork out of updating your syllabus. Our system acts as your personal AI assistant—reading your current course material, highlighting what's outdated (like old engine repair), and suggesting exactly what new topics (like Electric Vehicles) to teach.

🏢

For Companies & Employers

You no longer have to wait years for colleges to catch up with your technology. You can directly view proposed syllabi on our platform, vote on what's missing, and pledge to hire graduates who complete the courses you approved.

🏛️

For Government Planners

Stop guessing how many training seats to allocate. We predict the future hiring demand in your specific district and tell you exactly how many training seats you need, helping you prevent both skill shortages and graduate unemployment.

The Core Problem & Breakthrough

Why Traditional Skill Development Fails

Vocational and technical training institutes in India historically refresh their syllabi once every 5 to 8 years. Meanwhile, emerging tech cycles change in months, leaving graduates unemployed and industries starved of talent.

✕

The Traditional Vocational Lag

  • ⚠️
    Static Curricula: Syllabi still teach obsolete mechanical carburetors, analogue meters, and drafting boards while companies hire for EV BMS, SCADA, and 5-axis Cobots.
  • ⚠️
    District Capacity Blindspots: Training institutes produce hundreds of generic graduates without tracking local industrial hiring absorption, causing severe regional skill surpluses and shortages.
  • ⚠️
    Disconnected Industry Employers: Industry validation occurs via ad-hoc annual meetings with no structured quantitative voting, CAPEX lab sharing, or pledged job placement quotas.
  • ⚠️
    Generic Candidate Pathways: Students receive arbitrary certificate credentials without personalized placement probability or actionable bridge-course recommendations.
✓

The SkillPulse AI Solution Loop

  • ⚡
    Dynamic Semantic Drift Engine: Continuous TF-IDF vectorization matches syllabi against real-time job vacancy signals, flagging obsolete units and calculating a 0–100% Relevance Score.
  • ⚡
    Predictive District Training Plans: Random Forest regressor forecasts 3-year local demand, generating itemized lab equipment tenders (₹ Lakhs) and trainer upskilling quotas.
  • ⚡
    360° Employer Co-Design Workbench: Industry leaders (Tata Motors, Ather, Infosys, Adani Solar) review curricula in real-time, vote on module efficacy, and pledge hiring seats.
  • ⚡
    Precision Career Navigator: Machine learning classifier calculates candidate placement probability (82.7% accuracy) and prescribes custom bridge courses for target job roles.

Distinctive Innovations

Unique Selling Points (USPs)

Designed specifically to meet the high standards of the Smart India Hackathon jury with quantitative rigor and immediate governance impact.

🧠 USP 1: ML-Driven

Dynamic Semantic Skill-Drift & Obsolescence Engine

Replaces subjective syllabus reviews with rigorous natural language processing. The engine vectorizes existing syllabus text across 1,157 n-grams and computes cosine similarity against sector-specific industrial demand centroids trained on 10,500+ live job vacancies.

  • 📊 Relevance Index: Generates a calibrated 0–100% curriculum alignment score.
  • 🔍 Displacement Rationale: Explicitly flags deprecated units (e.g. Carburetor repair, Analogue meters) with clear industrial justification.
  • ⚡ 1-Click Modernization: Auto-injects emerging NSQF Level 5/6 competencies (e.g., EV BMS, SCADA, Cobots).
Algorithm: TF-IDF + Cosine Distance
Vocabulary: 1,157 Unigrams & Bigrams
Latency: < 45ms per syllabus
🗺️ USP 2: Governance-Driven

District Training Planner & 360° Employer Validation

Empowers District Skill Committees (DSCs) with predictive planning tools rather than reactive firefighting. Translates economic capital expenditure announcements into localized hiring demand forecasts across 16 industrial hubs.

  • 📈 3-Year Random Forest Forecast: Predicts hiring volume trajectory and mismatch seats.
  • 🛠️ Itemized Tenders (₹ Lakhs): Generates exact equipment specifications and budgetary estimates for ITI lab modernization.
  • 🤝 Employer Co-Design Workbench: Real-time upvoting, downvoting, and hiring seat pledges from Tata Motors, Ather, Infosys, etc.
Coverage: 16+ Tier 1/2 Industrial Districts
Forecast Horizon: 3-Year Rolling Outlook
Regressors: Random Forest (100 Estimators)
👨‍🎓 USP 3: Candidate-Centric

Precision Career Pathway & Placement Predictor

Empowers trainees and job seekers with empirical data on their employability. Candidates evaluate their skill profile against live industry requirements to identify competency deficits and projected wage premiums.

  • 🎯 Readiness Probability: Computes probability of securing placement within 90 days of graduation.
  • 📚 Targeted Bridge Modules: Prescribes specific micro-credentials needed to achieve >85% placement certainty.
  • 💰 Wage Premium Forecast: Real-time salary insights based on NSQF level and acquired competencies.
Classifier: Random Forest Classifier
Accuracy: 82.70% (ROC-AUC: 0.8093)
Trained On: 5,000 Verified Outcomes

REST API Endpoints Specification

Quickstart & Execution Guide

How to run the full triple-service SkillPulse AI ecosystem locally from scratch. 

# 1. Clone repository and install dependencies across all services
git clone <repo-url> sih_2
cd sih_2
npm run install-all

# 2. Seed MongoDB with baseline curricula, vacancies, and district data
npm run seed

# 3. (Optional) Re-train Machine Learning models on 18,000+ dataset records
cd ml_service
python train_models.py
cd ..

# 4. Start all three microservices concurrently (Client, Server, ML Engine)
npm start

# Service Port Bindings:
# -> Frontend Web UI:  http://localhost:5173  (Light/Dark theme)
# -> Express REST API: http://localhost:5000  (Node.js server)
# -> FastAPI ML Engine: http://localhost:8000  (Swagger docs: /docs)
